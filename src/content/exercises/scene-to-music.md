### Scene To Music

* Objective: Play a silent scene where players’ emotions are controlled by the music  
* Instructions:   
  * Two or three improvisers and one accompanist needed  
  * The actors must be directly influenced by the music being played  
  * The actors can’t speak, but must act in ways emotionally connected to the music  
* Example:  
  * Singer: “Can I get a suggestion of anything at all?”  
  * Audience: “Chainsaw”  
  * \*Accompanist starts playing upbeat music\*  
  * \*Actor 1 walks around happily\*  
  * \*Accompanist starts playing darker music\*  
  * \*Actor 2 brings out a chainsaw menacingly\*  
  * \*Keep the scene going until some narrative climax occurs\*  
* Notes:  
  * The accompanist should change the music pretty frequently.  
  * The actors should “lead with” their emotion and perform actions that resonate with that emotion.  
  * The actors will have to fill a lot in, but also be quickly response to music changes.
