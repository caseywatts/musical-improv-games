### Sung Story Die

* Objective: Players take turns collectively singing a story, trying not to mess up  
* Instructions:   
  * Many singers, one host, and one accompanist needed  
  * The host will point at a person to indicate they should be singing  
  * As soon as the host points at a person, they pick up right where the song was  
  * If a singer doesn’t keep the song’s flow, they are eliminated  
  * The last singer standing wins  
* Example:  
  * Host: “Can I get a suggestion of anything at all?”  
  * Audience: “Swimming”  
  * Host: “We present to you a song about swimming”  
  * \*Accompanist starts playing\*  
  * \*Host points to singer 1\*  
  * Singer 1: “There once was a woman, who wanted to learn how to…”  
  * \*Host points to singer 4\*  
  * Singer 4: “...learn how to swim. She went to take lessons…”  
  * \*Host points to singer 2\*  
  * Singer 2: “...lessons from…”  
  * \*Host eliminates singer 2\*  
  * \*Accompanist starts playing again\*  
  * \*Host points to singer 3\*  
  * Singer 3: “After she took those lessons, she wanted to test out…”  
  * \*Keep going until one singer is left\*  
* Notes:  
  * Make sure when you are pointed at, you pick up EXACTLY where the song left off; don’t repeat a word nor use a word that would be grammatically incorrect.  
  * If someone is eliminated, don’t argue; just exit gracefully.
