### Balladeer

* Objective: One person sings a ballad while other players act out the song as it is sung  
* Instructions:   
  * One singing improviser, two or three actors, and one accompanist needed  
  * The singer sings a ballad based on an audience suggestion, painting a story  
  * The actors play what the singer is singing about, in slow motion  
* Example:  
  * Singer: “Can I get a suggestion of anything at all?”  
  * Audience: “Bike”  
  * \*Accompanist starts playing\*  
  * Singer: “Once there was a bike so looooooong”  
  * Actors: \*Pantomime getting on a long bike\*  
  * Singer: “And a bike rider so strooooooong”  
  * Actors: \*One flexes to indicate they will play the biker\*  
  * \*Keep the ballad going until some narrative climax occurs\*  
* Notes:  
  * The singer should give rich imagery and action in their song.  
  * The actors should be a bit dramatic in their acting and should pay attention to all the details conveyed by the singer.  
  * The singer affects the actors, but the actors should influence the singer, too.
