### Ping Pong Ballad

* Objective: Have two people take turns singing about an audience member.  
* Instructions:   
  * Two singers, and audience volunteer, and one accompanist needed  
  * The singers do a quick interview of the audience member to learn about them  
  * The singers then sing about the audience member, but only one at a time  
  * The singer that the audience member is looking at is the one who sings  
* Example:  
  * Singers: \*Interview audience member\*  
  * Singer 1: \*Tell audience member that they determine who sings\*  
  * \*Accompanist starts playing\*  
  * Singer 1: “You are a mechanical engineer, you love to…”  
  * \*Audience member looks at Singer 2\*  
  * Singer 2: “...build everything that you own. You used to play with Hot Wheels when you were younger, now you play…”  
  * \*Audience member looks at Singer 1\*  
  * Singer 1: “... with blueprints when you’re grown.”  
  * \*Keep the song going until some narrative climax occurs\*  
* Notes:  
  * Make sure to get some key points of the audience member’s life as a  way to inspire the song.  
  * A singer should pick up right where the other left off, so that their sentences are grammatically correct.
